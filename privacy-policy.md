# Privacy Policy — Glintstone

Effective date: July 16, 2026

Glintstone is built to collect as little as possible. In short: everything you record stays on your device, and we do not collect, track, or share any of it.

## 1. Information we collect

**None.** Glintstone has no accounts and no sign-up. Everything you create in the app — your activities, notes, `#tags`, and their times — is stored only on your device.

## 2. On-device storage

Your records are saved locally on your device. We have no servers and never receive a copy. Deleting the app removes all of its data from your device.

## 3. No tracking or analytics

We do not use any analytics, advertising, or tracking tools. We do not track you across other apps or websites, and we do not collect device identifiers or your location.

## 4. Purchases

Glintstone offers a single one-time purchase (“Full Ruler”). All payments are handled by Apple through the App Store. We never see or receive your payment details — the app only learns whether the purchase is active. Apple’s handling of your purchase is governed by Apple’s own privacy policy.

## 5. Data sharing

We do not sell, rent, or share your data — because we do not collect it in the first place.

## 6. Children

Glintstone does not collect personal data from anyone, including children.

## 7. Data retention and deletion

Your data remains on your device until you delete individual records or uninstall the app. Because we hold nothing, there is nothing for us to retain or delete on our side.

## 8. Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, we will post the updated version here with a new effective date.
